What we build

Software for people who collect.

Every application we publish shares the same starting position: your data belongs on your own computer, not on a server you have to trust. No accounts, no cloud sync you didn’t ask for, and no business model built on holding a copy of what you own.

Watch Vault Archive is the first. The same architecture — encrypted, offline, built around provenance and long-term record-keeping rather than a feed — is built to serve other collector communities the same way, as they come.
